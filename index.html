Welcome

A nurturing start for every child.

Our promise is in our name: we're here to help your little one shine. We work in partnership with families to give every child a safe, secure place to be cared for, encouraged and nurtured, building on their unique skills and interests from the very start.

  • A key person for your child

    A familiar face who gets to know your child and helps them settle in gently.

  • Learning through play

    Following the EYFS, with daily feedback and ideas to try at home on Tapestry.

  • Freshly prepared meals

    Every meal is made on site, from breakfast through to tea.

  • Inclusive SEN support

    Needs are spotted early, and support is shaped with parents and professionals.

Our rooms

A room for every stage.

Children move through three rooms as they grow, each set up for their age and stage.

Babies

The Baby Room

A calm, cosy room for our youngest children, with soft spaces to rest and low furniture for first explorations.

Discover the room →

Younger 2s

The Garden Room

Home to our two-year-olds, a space made for busy hands, first friendships and growing independence.

Discover the room →

Older 2s to 5 years

The Toy Box

Our preschool room. Two to threes use one half, and over threes the other, as they get ready for school.

Discover the room →

Life at Gadebridge

Busy days, full of play.

We follow the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS). Children learn through a mix of child-led and adult-led play: exploring, having a go and discovering their own ideas.
